Original PDFs on this device

The library saves every original PDF on this device automatically the first time it is opened online (about 120 MB in all), so search results can show pictures of the real pages offline. Documents are grouped by topic: first aid and medical care, water and sanitation, food (canning, storage and raising), wild plants, survival and shelter, preparedness and home safety, and diabetes. USDA canning Guides 1–5 show their text; for the other documents the extracted text can scramble tables, so results show a picture of the original page. Each document keeps its source, date and rights, and many carry a caution (for example, outdated advice) shown with every result. Most are U.S. Government works. The IFRC Shelter Safety Handbook is shared for noncommercial use with attribution, not as site-specific engineering advice; the University of Kentucky and Mississippi State extension guides allow copying for nonprofit, educational use with credit. These are separate references, not blended instructions or a personal treatment plan. Browser storage can still be cleared by you or by the device, so also keep a portable copy (see below) or the PDFs in Files or Books.

Keep a copy that never needs the internet

On a Mac or PC, copy the whole library folder (including the originals and vendor folders) to the computer or a USB drive and double-click index.html. Search works straight from the files with no internet, no server and no browser cache; “Open page” opens the PDF in the browser’s own PDF reader. Page pictures need the installed app.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari can’t run a library saved in the Files app. Use the Home Screen app, and also save the PDFs in Files or Books, which can search inside PDFs offline.

Import more TXT guides

Select one or more .txt files from Files. Unzip collections first. Imports stay on this device and are not uploaded.

Safety reminder: Text extraction can scramble canning tables. Check processing times, pressure, altitude, and jar sizes in the original USDA page before use. PDFs are references, not a complete emergency plan. No AI or online service is used by this search app.
