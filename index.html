Original PDFs on this device

The library saves every original PDF on this device automatically the first time it is opened online, so search results can show pictures of the real pages offline. Introduction, Guides 6–7 and the CDC/USGS documents show a picture of the original page because their extracted text can scramble tables; Guides 1–5 show their text. The library also includes the IFRC Shelter Safety Handbook (2011), illustrated shelter-risk and safer-construction guidance shared for noncommercial use with attribution, not as site-specific engineering advice; and CDC diabetes emergency preparation, insulin management and DKA warning signs, plus FDA emergency insulin guidance and its product-specific insulin reference. Webpage captures are labeled separately from original PDFs. These are separate references, not blended instructions or a personal treatment plan. Browser storage can still be cleared by you or by the device, so also keep a portable copy (see below) or the PDFs in Files or Books.

Keep a copy that never needs the internet

On a Mac or PC, copy the whole library folder (including the originals and vendor folders) to the computer or a USB drive and double-click index.html. Search works straight from the files with no internet, no server and no browser cache; “Open page” opens the PDF in the browser’s own PDF reader. Page pictures need the installed app.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari can’t run a library saved in the Files app. Use the Home Screen app, and also save the PDFs in Files or Books, which can search inside PDFs offline.

Import more TXT guides

Select one or more .txt files from Files. Unzip collections first. Imports stay on this device and are not uploaded.

Safety reminder: Text extraction can scramble canning tables. Check processing times, pressure, altitude, and jar sizes in the original USDA page before use. PDFs are references, not a complete emergency plan. No AI or online service is used by this search app.
